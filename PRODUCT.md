# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Technical SEO practitioners and the engineers who own the site. They arrive with a
specific suspicion ("why doesn't the model know what we do?") and read the audit
beside their own HTML, at a desk, in daylight, for a long sitting. They compare,
copy evidence IDs, and follow a claim back to the page that should have carried it.
They are not browsing. They are looking something up.

## Product Purpose

SPECTRA measures what an AI system actually understands about a website, and shows
where information dies between the page and the answer. Its claim is provenance:
every number traces to a source record, and a missing fact can be located at the
stage that lost it (crawl, extraction, semantic graph, model understanding,
retrieval). Success is a reader who can say "this failed here, because of this
evidence" without trusting us.

## Brand Personality

Exact, unembellished, accountable. It states measurements and shows its working.
It never estimates in the voice of a fact, and it never decorates a number.
Three words: instrument, ledger, candour.

## Anti-references

- **Dark SaaS** (Linear / Vercel dark): navy ground, cyan or violet accent, rounded
  panels on slightly-lighter panels, soft glows. This is what SPECTRA looked like
  and what it must not return to.
- **Analytics dashboards** (Grafana / Datadog / Mixpanel): radial gauges, KPI tiles,
  a score in a ring, sparkline widgets.
- **Marketing landing pages**: hero plus three feature cards plus a "how it works"
  1-2-3 strip.
- **Warm-paper editorial**: cream, sand, bone or parchment grounds with a brass or
  oxblood accent. The second-order reflex for "technical tool that isn't dark".

## Design Principles

1. **Provenance over presentation.** If a number is on screen, the path to its
   evidence is on screen too. Nothing is shown that cannot be traced.
2. **The instrument does not flatter the reading.** No gauge, no ring, no colour
   that makes a bad score look designed. A failure should look like a failure.
3. **Colour is data or it is absent.** The page is achromatic. One signal colour
   carries one meaning. State is otherwise encoded in weight and value, which
   survives greyscale, projection and colour blindness.
4. **Rules and type, not containers.** Grouping comes from hairlines, alignment and
   space. A card must earn its elevation; on this product almost none do.
5. **Density is a service, not a cost.** This reader wants more on screen, not less.
   Airiness that pushes the third measurement below the fold is a failure of nerve.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Body text at or above 4.5:1, large text at or above 3:1, verified
rather than assumed. State is never colour-only: every signal-coloured element
carries a text label or a weight difference that reads in greyscale. Full keyboard
path with a visible focus ring on an achromatic ground. `prefers-reduced-motion`
honoured on every transition. Target size 24px minimum, 44px on coarse pointers.
